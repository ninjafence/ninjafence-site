NinjaDCC

NinjaDCC is a wireless dust collection control system built around three coordinated devices. The Tool detects when a woodworking machine starts or stops, the optional Gate opens the correct extraction branch and reports its position, and the Collector combines demand from multiple Tools to control the dust collector automatically.
